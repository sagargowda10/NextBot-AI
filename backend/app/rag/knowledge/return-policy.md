# Return and Refund Policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 10 days of delivery. The return window starts on the date the order is marked as delivered. Requests made after the window has closed cannot be accepted.

## Eligible items
Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all tags, accessories and the invoice included. Innerwear, personal-care products and gift cards are not returnable for hygiene and security reasons.

## How to request a return
Open My Orders, choose the delivered order and select Request Return, or simply tell the NextBot assistant "I want to return my order". The assistant checks eligibility instantly and creates the return request.

## Approval and pickup
Returns with a refund value below ₹10,000 are approved automatically. Returns at or above ₹10,000 are reviewed by our team, usually within one working day. After approval, a courier pickup is scheduled within 1 to 2 working days.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned item passes a quality check at our warehouse. UPI and wallet refunds usually reflect within 2 to 3 working days; card and net-banking refunds take 5 to 7 working days depending on your bank. Shipping fees are refunded only if the item was damaged, defective or not as described.

## Damaged or wrong items
If you receive a damaged, defective or wrong item, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund including shipping fees.
