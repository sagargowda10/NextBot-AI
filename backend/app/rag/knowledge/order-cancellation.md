# Order Cancellation Policy

## When can I cancel?
You can cancel an order any time before it is packed. Once an order is packed or shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but you can request a return after delivery.

## How to cancel
Open My Orders and choose Cancel Order, or ask the assistant to cancel your latest order. Reserved stock is released immediately.

## Refunds for cancelled orders
If you already paid, the full amount is refunded to the original payment method. Orders of ₹50,000 or more are cancelled after a quick review by our team, usually within a few hours.
