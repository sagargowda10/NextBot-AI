# Account, Coupons and Offers

## Coupons
Enter a coupon code in the cart or checkout page, or tell the assistant the code when you place your order. Only one coupon can be used per order. WELCOME10 gives 10 percent off your first order up to ₹1,000 on orders above ₹1,500. FLAT500 gives ₹500 off orders above ₹4,999.

## Resetting your password
Use the Forgot Password link on the login page. For your security, our support team and the AI assistant can never see or reset your password for you.

## Managing addresses
You can save multiple delivery addresses in your profile and mark one as default. The default address is used when you place an order through the assistant.
