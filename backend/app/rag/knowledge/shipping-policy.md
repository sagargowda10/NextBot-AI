# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Shipping charges
Shipping is free on orders of ₹999 or more after discounts. Orders below ₹999 have a flat shipping fee of ₹79.

## Delivery timelines
All orders ship from our Bengaluru fulfilment centre. Delivery within Bengaluru (PIN codes starting with 56) is next day. Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana take 1 to 2 days. Tamil Nadu and Kerala take 2 to 3 days. Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat and Rajasthan take 3 to 5 days. North, East and North-East India take 4 to 6 days. You can ask the assistant for an estimate for any 6-digit PIN code.

## Order processing
Orders placed before 2 PM on working days are packed the same day. You receive a tracking number by email (and WhatsApp, if enabled) as soon as the shipment is created.

## Tracking
Track any order from My Orders or by asking the assistant "Track my latest order". The tracking timeline shows when the order was packed, shipped, out for delivery and delivered.

## Failed delivery attempts
The courier makes up to three delivery attempts. If all attempts fail, the order is returned to our warehouse and a full refund is issued to the original payment method.
