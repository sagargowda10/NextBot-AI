# Warranty Information

## Manufacturer warranty
Laptops and smartphones come with a one-year manufacturer warranty. Audio products and wearables have a six-month warranty. Footwear and apparel are covered against manufacturing defects for 30 days from delivery.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use. It does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repairs or normal wear and tear.

## How to claim warranty
Keep your invoice, which is available in My Orders. Contact our support team with the order number and a short description of the problem. We will help you arrange a repair or replacement through the brand's authorised service centre.

## Extended warranty
Extended warranty plans are available for selected laptops at checkout and add one extra year of coverage.
