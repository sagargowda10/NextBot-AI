# Payment FAQ

## Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards and net banking through our payment partners. In demo mode, payments are processed by a simulated gateway and no real money is charged.

## Is it safe to pay on NextBot?
Yes. Card details are entered on the payment partner's secure page and are never stored on our servers. We do not store card numbers or CVV codes.

## My payment failed but money was deducted
If a payment fails after your account was debited, the amount is automatically reversed by your bank, usually within 5 to 7 working days. Your items stay reserved for 30 minutes, so you can retry the payment from My Orders.

## Can I retry a failed payment?
Yes. Open the order in My Orders and choose Pay Now, or tell the assistant "Place my order" again. After three failed attempts the order is cancelled and the reserved stock is released.

## Why is my order under review?
For your protection, a small number of orders are reviewed by our team before payment, for example unusually large orders or several failed payment attempts in a short time. Reviews usually finish within a few hours and no money is taken until the review is complete.

## Do you offer EMI?
No-cost EMI is available on selected cards for orders above ₹10,000 through our payment partner.
