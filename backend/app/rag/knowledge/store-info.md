# About NextBot Store

## Who we are
NextBot Store is a fictional demonstration online retailer used to showcase the NextBot AI platform. It sells electronics, footwear, apparel, wearables and home and kitchen products from the brands Voltix, Nimbus, Auralis, Stridex and HearthCo.

## Customer support hours
Our support team is available from 9 AM to 9 PM IST, Monday to Saturday. The AI assistant is available around the clock and can hand any conversation to a human during support hours.

## Contact
Email support@nextbot.local. Escalated chats receive a reply within 4 working hours.

## Fulfilment centre
Orders are fulfilled from our Bengaluru fulfilment centre, with a secondary warehouse in Mysuru for footwear and apparel.
